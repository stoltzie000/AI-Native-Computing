This table explicitly records when requested measurements **do not exist in the inspected source evidence**. The absence of reported numbers in our review is not a proof no benchmark exists elsewhere. A theoretical peak, network link rate, RTL simulation counter, measured FPGA result and measured application GPU comparison are **different quantities**.

## Required missing-data gate
For *each shortlisted physical contender*, measure or retrieve **with the same workload and task quality**: exact memory capacity and physically sustained bandwidth, p50/p95 end-to-end latency, batch throughput, wattmeter-defined idle/load/joules per accepted task, kernel/precision configuration, multi-node scaling and failure rate. If any field remains unavailable, label it **UNKNOWN**, not zero and not comparable.

The evidence in this file is from **source inspection**; aside from our project's prior RTL and tiny-checkpoint audits, these external devices and reports have not been independently exercised in our environment.
