# Expanded ideas and mechanism-level originality register — 2026-11-23

**Design principle:** We are free to modify processor, ISA, training representation, memory, motherboard/backplane, photonics, and AI execution order. Do not assume newness implies higher task capability or fewer joules. These are **experiments**, not architectures approved for production.

## Physical feasibility from published work: key opportunities
- [IBM NorthPole physical chip](https://research.ibm.com/publications/neural-inference-at-the-frontier-of-energy-space-and-time) shows close memory-compute integration can materially change *measured image-inference* economics, but capacity limits and full task breadth matter.
- [Taalas HC1](https://taalas.com/products/) physically validates model-specific silicon as a product class; it doesn't solve flexibility or prove its vendor throughput under our benchmark.
- [IBM PCM ALBERT](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63794-4) is evidence of physical analog transformer execution, including nontrivial drift/recalibration.
- [Photonic tensor processor](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71599-2) establishes physically executed optical classification, not unconstrained modern LLM inference.
- [Loihi 2/Hala Point](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/intel-builds-worlds-largest-neuromorphic-system.html) demonstrates real asynchronous/event neuromorphic hardware; best first comparison is event-driven workloads.
- [Taalas](https://taalas.com/products/), [Cerebras WSE-3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2021728/000162828026029503/cerebras-sx1amay2026.htm), [Tenstorrent](https://github.com/tenstorrent/tt-metal) and [Google TPU7x](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/compute-area/tpus) are important non-GPU physical comparators with different strengths.

## Why this project must not just reproduce prior art
1. Building another systolic PE or a conventional SRAM buffer without a distinct, **measured** benefit is **DUPLICATE** of established accelerator research; borrow Gemmini/openTPU methods first.
2. Simply equipping our design with ~22TB/s HBM4 does not beat [Rubin's announced 22TB/s peak](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/hgx/). Quality-matched work/joule is the target.
3. Our v0.6/v0.7 natural zero-*weight* bypass has no work to eliminate on two genuinely trained checkpoints measured in [our audit](../../experiments/real_weight_audit/results/MEASURED_2026-11-23.json). Structured training remains a separate research opportunity.
4. Our 64-row v1.0 buffered PE passed 760 RTL test cases but has **no actual SRAM macro, physical HBM/controller, achieved clock, measured energy or full inference**. Avoid promoting generic Yosys cell counts to FPGA power or claimed superiority.
5. All unusual representation or operator proposals require a **reproducible quality/utility oracle**; arithmetic savings achieved by changing the task or tolerating worse answers are not a valid win.

## Strongest near-term crossover experiment
**Compare on an identical FPGA:** Gemmini-style tightly coupled scratchpad/control baseline vs v1.0 column-buffered/gated PE vs an activation-gated coalescing variant, keeping arithmetic format, number of MACs, memory link/controller, input model and power measurement identical. Start with physically present INT8 layer kernels, then extend to an end-to-end task. Retain a no-activation-sparsity workload as a *negative control*. Only continue accelerator-specific optimization if throughput/energy benefit survives.
